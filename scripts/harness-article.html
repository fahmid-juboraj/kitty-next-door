Why cats sit on keyboards

Cats are drawn to warm surfaces, and a laptop keyboard is warm, slightly soft, and right where your attention is. Sitting there is a reliable way to be noticed.

Researchers who study feline behavior point out that cats also like elevated, central spots from which they can watch their people. Your desk checks every box.

If you want your cat off the keyboard, offer a warm alternative nearby: a folded blanket or a heated pad a little to the side of your screen.
