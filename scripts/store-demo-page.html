Small things that make long workdays lighter

5 min read

Working at a computer all day can feel quiet in a way that's hard to describe. Little rituals help: a warm drink, a window cracked open, a playlist you know by heart.

Some people keep a plant on their desk. Others keep a photo of someone they miss. Anything that reminds you that you're not alone in the room makes a difference.

And every so often, it's nice when a friend drops by, even just to say hello.
