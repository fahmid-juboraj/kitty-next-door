A little cat that keeps you company.

A small friend for long days at the computer

No feeding schedule, no guilt. It just keeps you company.

🐾

Lives on your screen

Walks, sits, loafs and naps along the bottom of every page. Clicks pass straight through it.

👀

Notices you

Its eyes follow your cursor. Step away and it curls up to sleep; come back and it wakes up to greet you.

💌

Visits your friends

Send it with a note and a little gift. It walks off your screen and onto theirs, in real time.

🔒

Private by design

It never reads the pages you visit or what you type. Only names, friends and visit notes are shared.

How visits work

Friends add each other once. After that it's one click.

Swap friend codes

Everyone gets a short code like 7K2F-9QXM. Share it once with a friend.

Press Send

Add a note and a gift. Your cat walks off the edge of your screen.

It shows up for them

It sits with their cat and delivers your note, then comes home a couple of hours later, or whenever you call it.

Get your cat

Free, and it works in the browser you already use.
